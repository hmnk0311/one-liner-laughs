One one-line joke (123 characters): a gym with a money-back guarantee pays its own customer out in week two, unprompted, with a note that just says "we saw" — the business gives up on the customer before the customer gives up on the gym. The program writes that single line to /output/entry.txt. Fourteen candidates were written for this entry and put through two rounds of strict external review against the judge's published brief (a real one-line joke with setup and payoff, a laugh out loud rather than a smile, a surprise twist, freshness, and no wasted words); the review killed the memory-foam pillow, the loyal student debt, the self-employed boss and the alarm clock that died in its sleep as recycled or predictable, and four evidence-earning revisions of the gym line all lost head-to-head to the original, because stating what the gym saw delivers the joke early and leaves the closing note explaining it again.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
cat out/entry.txt
```
